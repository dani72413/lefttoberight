My story

I’m Daniel, 41. When I was 11, my family fled with a few hundred dollars and started over in America, in poverty. I didn’t want to burden my parents, who were working hard for us, so the streets became my home.

That led to youth detention, county jails and prisons, an attempt on my life, and more than 20 years apart from my family. There were mornings I woke up and picked up a gun. Today I wake up and start my day in prayer.

I found a spiritual path, built a new life, and today I’m alive, free of debt and without enemies. This book is the true story of how I got here.

From Chapter 1

I was thirteen years old, and I slept on the floor.

The room was small, with a worn carpet. On the wall hung a little basketball hoop, the kind you throw a soft, small ball into. A kid’s toy, in a kid’s room.

Nobody in the house knew I hadn’t really slept. Every time I closed my eyes, I saw the same thing: the barrel of a gun, inches from my face.
